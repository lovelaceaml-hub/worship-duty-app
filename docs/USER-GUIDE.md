# Welcome to LV Zion Worship Service

A simple guide to using the app — no technical background needed.

**The app:** https://lv-zion-duties.web.app

---

## Getting in

1. Open the link above on your phone or computer.
2. If you see a screen asking for a **4-digit passcode**, ask the person who
   shared the link with you for it, type it in, and tap **Enter**.
3. That's it — your device remembers it, so you won't be asked again unless
   the passcode changes.

Tip: on an iPhone, tap the Share icon and choose **Add to Home Screen**. This
makes the app open like a normal app icon, and it's required if you want to
receive notifications while the app is closed.

---

## Finding your duties

Tap **My Duties** at the bottom (or top) of the screen.

1. The first time, pick your name from the dropdown where it says
   **"Who are you?"** The app remembers your choice after that.
2. Once you've picked your name, you'll see a card for each upcoming duty
   you're assigned — the date, the time, which service, and which role
   (like "Usher Door" or "Media Booth #1").
3. Tap **"Who else is serving?"** on any card to see everyone else assigned
   to that same service.

### Turning on notifications

Tap **Turn on notifications** on My Duties and allow the browser's prompt.
Once it's on, you'll get an alert when:

- A new duty is assigned to you, or
- One of your duties is coming up within the next 24 hours.

### Adding duties to your calendar

Tap **Add my duties to my calendar**. Your phone subscribes to your own live
duty list, complete with a reminder the day before and again a couple of hours
ahead. Because it is a subscription rather than a one-time copy, it keeps
itself right: when a duty is reassigned, or a service time moves, your calendar
follows within about half a day. You do not need the app open for this.

On Android, tap **Copy calendar link** instead, then in Google Calendar go to
**Other calendars → From URL** and paste it.

## Planning songs (for presiders)

The song-planning tool that used to be a separate app now lives right inside
this one. You'll only see it if you're assigned as a **Presider** for an
upcoming service.

There are two ways in:

- **The Presider tab.** When you have an upcoming Presider duty, a fourth
  tab called **Presider** appears at the bottom, between My Duties and
  Manage. Tapping it opens the song planner on its **Home** view, showing
  your upcoming services. (No Presider duty coming up? The tab simply isn't
  there.)
- **Straight from a duty.** On **My Duties**, a button appears: **"Plan
  songs for your service."** (Each Presider role line also has a smaller
  **"Pick songs"** link.) These open the planner already on that service's
  setlist.

Once you're in the planner:

1. Use the **Library** to search the full song catalog and tap the round
   **+** next to a song to add it. Tap again to add it a second time. A blue
   ring means the song hasn't been sung recently; a grey ring means it has.
2. Swipe left/right on the song list to move between groups of 100 songs, or
   use the little arrows and dots.
3. Set the **Glory song** and (on Sabbath mornings) the **Thanks song** using
   the *Choose* buttons — these are shown in the order of worship but aren't
   counted in the setlist's total time.
4. **Home** inside the planner shows your upcoming services; **Services**
   shows everyone's; **Insights** shows which songs are overdue to be sung.
5. On the day of the service, a card's button becomes **Runsheet** — a big,
   easy-to-read order of worship for glancing at during the service.
6. After the service, open **Services** and tap **Mark as sung** so the song
   counts stay accurate. The first time anyone does an editor action, the app
   asks you to create a short **editor PIN** — this protects marking songs
   sung, undo, and backfill.
7. Tap **"← My Duties"** at the top (or any other bottom tab) any time to go
   back to the rest of the app.

Song picks that presiders post in the church Signal group are still read
automatically and added here — nothing about that changed.

*If you had the old song app saved to your phone, its icon now sends you
straight to this combined app.*

If you're the scheduler and ever want to hide this feature, turn off
**"Presider song picker"** under **Manage → Features**.

## The Schedule tab

Tap **Schedule** to see every upcoming service for everyone, grouped by day.
The app opens on the next upcoming service in Pacific time. As soon as a
service's start time arrives, the default advances to the next one, even if
that is later the same day or the next day. You can still choose an earlier
date or service yourself. Original shows the focused service above the full list;
Prism, Navy & Cream, and Aurora select it in their date and time controls.

When the app is temporarily in maintenance, the normal controls are hidden.
The maintenance page still shows the next eight services with dates, times,
and posted duties as a read-only schedule.

- Each card shows the service (e.g. "Sabbath Day · Morning"), the time,
  and who's assigned to each role, grouped by department (Worship, Usher
  Sisters, Usher Brothers, Lobby, Translation, Media, Parking Lot, Offering).
- If a choir song has been chosen for that service, it shows on a line near
  the top of the card.
- **Department chips** sit above the list — tap one (like "Usher Sisters")
  to narrow the whole Schedule tab down to just that department's duties
  for every date. Tap **All** to go back to seeing everything.
- On a phone the main page tabs float at the bottom of the screen in every
  appearance, always within thumb reach; on a wide screen they stay at the
  top. Use the Appearance button in the top controls at any time to choose
  Original, Prism, Navy & Cream, or Aurora.
- Tap **"Show past services"** near the bottom if you want to look back at
  services that already happened.

## Requesting a schedule change

If you're assigned a duty you need to swap or drop:

1. Go to **My Duties** and find the duty card in question.
2. Tap **Request change**.
3. A small note box appears — write a short reason if you'd like (optional),
   then tap **Submit**.
4. The card now shows **"Request pending"** instead of the button, so you
   know it went through.
5. The scheduler is notified automatically. Once they've decided, you'll see
   the request's status change to **Approved** or **Declined** under
   "Your requests" on My Duties, and you'll get a notification too.

Note: approving a request doesn't automatically change the schedule by
itself — the scheduler still has to go make the actual assignment change,
so don't assume you're off the hook until you see the new assignment
reflected on Schedule or My Duties.

## For the scheduler / editor

Everything below needs the separate **editor passcode** — ask whoever
manages the app if you don't have it. Tap **Manage** to get started.

### Editing assignments

1. In Manage, go to **Services** and tap a service to expand it.
2. Each role has a dropdown. Pick a name and it saves instantly for
   everyone.
3. The dropdown shows only active department members who are eligible for the
   duty, schedulable, and available for that service time and date.
4. A date exception takes priority over recurring availability. Administrators
   can use the clearly labeled override with a required reason when necessary.

### Managing department rosters

In **Manage**, choose a department from Leader Configuration, then open its
department page:

- The roster contains active and inactive members for that department only.
- A department leader can change active status, eligible duties,
  schedulability, recurring availability, and date exceptions only for a
  department they lead.
- Each saved field shows who last changed it and when.
- To add someone, search for them by name in **Add a department member** and
  choose **Add**. They need an existing member account. Adding a profile never
  grants a leader or administrator role.

### Updating your availability

Open **My Duties** after signing in. Your shared department profile includes
recurring service-time availability and date exceptions. You can edit only your
own availability. Your department leader edits the same record, and the latest
saved editor and time remain visible.

### Reviewing and approving requests

- A small red dot appears on the **Manage** tab whenever there's a pending
  change request.
- Go to **Manage → Requests** to see them, each with an **Approve**,
  **Decline**, or **Open service** button. "Open service" jumps straight to
  that service in Services so you can make the actual reassignment.
- Past requests (approved or declined) are kept in a collapsible history
  further down the page.
- Pick who receives these notifications in **Manage → Features →
  Scheduler** — choose a name from the dropdown there.

### One-time "Unify member names" button

If someone's name has multiple spellings floating around from before (like
"B. Anthony L" showing up alongside "B. Anthony"), there's a cleanup button
in **Manage → Data & Sync → Unify member names**. It merges all the known
variant spellings into one canonical name, everywhere — member list, every
service assignment, and your own device history if it applies to you. Safe
to run more than once; it only changes what still needs fixing.

If you also use the Google Sheet to enter the schedule, run the sheet's own
**Duty Tools → Unify member names** menu item *first*, then come back here
and do **Sync now** followed by **Unify member names**, in that order.

### Sync now

If you keep the schedule in the shared Google Sheet, the app checks it
automatically every few minutes — but if you just made a change and don't
want to wait, go to **Manage → Data & Sync** and tap **Sync now**. It'll
tell you what changed (services added/updated, new people found) and flag
anything it didn't understand.

### Choosing which features are on

**Manage → Features** lets you turn things on or off for everyone at once —
no waiting, no separate step for each device. This includes the site
passcode, the editor passcode, notification types, the calendar button,
double-duty warnings, Google Sheet sync, and change requests. There's also
where you change either passcode and pick the scheduler.

---

That's the whole app. If something looks wrong or missing, check with
whoever manages the Manage tab — most day-to-day fixes just need a tap in
Services, People, or Data & Sync.
