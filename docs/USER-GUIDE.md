# LV Zion Worship Service: Member Guide

A simple guide to using the app. No technical background needed.

**The app:** https://lv-zion-duties.web.app

Last updated: 2026-09-20. Leaders and administrators: see the
[User Manual](USER-MANUAL.md) for Manage.

---

## 1. Getting in

1. Open https://lv-zion-duties.web.app on your phone or computer.
2. If you see **"This schedule is private"**, type the 4-digit passcode and tap
   **Enter**. Ask the person who shared the link if you do not have it. Your
   device remembers it until the passcode changes.
3. On **Member sign in**, choose your name, type your member PIN (4 to 6
   digits), and tap **Sign in**.
4. If the app says **"Change your member PIN"**, type a new PIN twice and tap
   **Save PIN**. This happens after a leader resets your PIN.

You stay signed in on that device until you sign out.

Forgot your PIN? Ask your department leader or an administrator to reset it.
You will choose a new one the next time you sign in.

Tip for iPhone: tap the Share icon, then **Add to Home Screen**. The app then
opens like a normal app, and this is required to get notifications while the
app is closed.

---

## 2. Finding your way around

### The top of the screen

Every screen has the same top bar:

- The **LV** badge and **LV ZION**. Tap it to go back to Schedule.
- The name of the screen you are on (Schedule, My Duties, Presider, or Manage).
- A small **palette icon**. This is the Appearance button (section 8).
- **Your name**. Tap it to open your menu (below).

On a phone in the Original and Navy & Cream looks, a small dot in the top bar
shows that the schedule is updating live.

### The bar at the bottom (phones)

On a phone, the main screens are a bar that floats at the bottom:
**Schedule**, **My Duties**, and, if they apply to you, **Presider** and
**Manage**. The bar hides while the keyboard is open. On a wide screen the same
buttons sit at the top instead.

### Your name menu

Tap your name at the top right. You get three choices:

1. **Availability**: tell your department when you can serve (section 5).
2. **Settings**: notifications, your calendar, and Appearance (section 6).
3. **Sign out**.

### The dots

- A **green dot on My Duties** means a new duty was added for you. Open My
  Duties and it clears.
- A **red dot on Manage** (leaders only) means a request or a scheduling
  conflict needs attention.

## 3. Schedule

Tap **Schedule** to see every upcoming service and who is serving.

- The app opens on the **next service**, in Pacific time. When a service's start
  time arrives, the app moves on to the one after it, even if that is later the
  same day. You can still pick an earlier date or service yourself.
- Each card shows the service, the time, and who has each duty, grouped by
  department.
- If a choir song is chosen for a service, it shows near the top of the card.
- **Department buttons** sit above the list. Tap one (like "Usher Sisters") to
  see only that department. Tap **All** to see everything again.
- Tap **Show past services** near the bottom to look back.

If you ever see **"Schedule maintenance in progress"**, the app is being
updated. You can still read the next eight services. It usually lasts about a
minute.

---

## 4. My Duties

Tap **My Duties** to see only your own duties.

1. Each card is one day: the date, the time, the service, and your duty.
2. Tap **Who else is serving?** to see everyone in that service.
3. **Recent updates** lists new duties added for you. Tap **Clear** to empty it.

### Asking for a change

1. On the duty, tap **Request change**.
2. Write a short reason if you want (optional), then tap **Submit**.
3. The duty now shows **Pending**.
4. The scheduler is notified. Under **Your requests** you will see
   **Approved** or **Declined** once they decide, and you get a notification.

Important: an approved request does not change the schedule by itself. The
scheduler still makes the change. You are covered only when Schedule or My
Duties shows the new name.

---

## 5. Availability

1. Tap your name at the top right, then **Availability**.
2. Under **Recurring service times**, set each service time (Morning,
   Afternoon, Evening, and so on) to **Available** or **Unavailable**.
3. Under **Date exceptions**, add a single date when you can or cannot serve.
   A date exception wins over the recurring choice for that day.
4. Tap **Save availability**. **Discard changes** puts it back.

Your department leader sees and can edit the same record. If the screen says
you are not on a department roster yet, ask your department leader to add you.

---

## 6. Settings, notifications, and your calendar

Tap your name, then **Settings**. The notification and calendar buttons are
also at the top of **My Duties**.

### Notifications

1. Tap **Turn on notifications** and allow the prompt.
2. You are alerted when a new duty is added for you, and reminded within 24
   hours before each duty.
3. To stop, tap **Notifications on**.

On iPhone, notifications with the app closed need the app on your Home Screen
(iOS 16.4 or later).

### Calendar

1. Tap **Add my duties to my calendar**.
2. Your phone subscribes to your own duty list, with a reminder the day before
   and again about two hours ahead.
3. It keeps itself correct: when a duty or a service time changes, your
   calendar follows within about half a day.

On Android: tap **How it works and Android steps**, tap **Copy calendar link**,
then in Google Calendar go to **Other calendars**, **From URL**, and paste it.

### Member PIN

To change your PIN, ask your department leader or an administrator to reset it.

## 7. Presider (song planning)

You see **Presider** only when you have an upcoming Presider duty (or you are a
song editor).

Two ways in:

- Tap **Presider** in the bottom bar. It opens on **Home** with your upcoming
  services.
- On **My Duties**, tap **Plan songs for your service**, or **Pick songs** on a
  Presider duty. These open that service's song list directly.

On a phone, **Home, Library, Services, Insights** are one row under the top
bar.

1. **Library**: search the song catalog and tap the round **+** to add a song.
   A blue ring means it has not been sung recently; a grey ring means it has.
2. Swipe the song list left or right to move between groups of 100 songs.
3. Set the **Glory song** and, on Sabbath mornings, the **Thanks song** with the
   **Choose** buttons.
4. When the list is right, tap **Done, these are my songs**. If you change it
   later, confirm it again. My Duties shows whether your songs are picked,
   changed, or confirmed.
5. **Home** shows your services (**My Services**) or everyone's
   (**All Services**). **Services** shows all upcoming and past song lists.
   **Insights** shows which songs are overdue.
6. On the day of the service, a **Runsheet** button gives a large, easy to read
   order of worship.
7. After the service, tap **Mark as sung** so the song counts stay right.

Song picks posted in the church Signal group are still added automatically.

## 8. Appearance

The app has four looks: **Original**, **Prism**, **Navy & Cream**, and
**Aurora**. They show the same schedule and do the same things.

1. Tap the small palette icon at the top (or your name, **Settings**,
   **Appearance**).
2. Pick a look. It applies to this device only.

Prism and Aurora switch to dark colors by themselves when your phone or
computer is in dark mode. Original and Navy & Cream always stay light. There is
no dark mode button and no pause button in the app. Animations turn off when
your device is set to reduce motion.

---

## 9. Sending feedback

After you sign in, a small round button with a speech bubble sits at the bottom
right, just above the bottom bar on a phone.

1. Tap it and pick one: **Feedback**, **Report a bug**, or **Suggest a change**.
2. Type your message (up to 2,000 characters). For a bug, say which screen you
   were on, what you did, and what you expected.
3. Tap **Send**. You will see "Thank you. Your feedback was sent."

If it does not send (for example, no signal), your message stays in the box and
the button changes to **Retry**. **Cancel** closes the box without sending, and
what you typed is still there next time. The button hides while the keyboard is
open. Only administrators can read what you send.

---

## 10. If something looks wrong

- A duty is missing: check that you signed in under your own name.
- You cannot sign in: ask your department leader or an administrator to reset
  your PIN.
- Anything else: use the feedback button (section 9).
