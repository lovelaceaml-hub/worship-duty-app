# LV Zion Worship Service prototype brief

## Purpose

This local prototype gives members, presiders, and schedule coordinators a clickable review of fictional September 2026 worship assignments. It carries the selected Open Bulletin visual language into Home, My Duties, Presider, and Manage while keeping the original whitespace, blue service card, serif headings, and phone and desktop composition.

## Product moments

1. Home shows the selected member, My next duty, the nearest expanded service, later services that open on request, real department labels, and personal highlighting.
2. My Duties filters assignments by department and date, shows past services when enabled, simulates calendar subscription and feed-link copying alongside a local calendar snapshot, and submits a fictional change request with request history.
3. Presider is available only for an actual Presider assignment. It switches among the selected member's services, shows the canonical outline, keeps duplicate song occurrences independent, excludes Glory and Thanks from preparation duration, and supports song search, details, history, add, remove, reorder, save, retry, confirm, changed-after-confirmation, runsheet, copy, sung, undo, backfill, and deletion.
4. Manage opens with pending requests and assignment editing. People supports add, rename, name-consolidation preview and confirmation, remove with future-duty impact confirmation, and department rosters. Planning derives conflicts, exemptions, workload, gaps, and a local suggestion. Sync and Settings cover the fictional sheet URL, strictly validated fictional backup imports, confirmed restore and reset, human feature labels, passcode changes, and route-wide locks.
5. Empty, loading, offline, error, save-failure, and success routes provide visible recovery results through the review toolbar.

## Fixed fictional sample

The prototype uses only B. Daniel Reed, S. Mira Lane, and B. Elias Park. Service records are Sabbath Day Saturday September 12 2026 at 9 AM, 3 PM, and 8 PM, plus Third Day Tuesday September 15 2026 at 7 PM. The departments are Worship, Usher Sisters, Usher Brothers, Lobby, Translation, Media, Parking Lot, and Offering. Duty labels match the existing product vocabulary, including Presider, Sermon, Prayer of Thanks, Closing Prayer, usher roles, Lobby Security, Translation, Media, Parking, and Offering roles.

## Boundaries

This is a local proposal artifact. It has no Firebase, spreadsheet, external network, production source connection, or remote asset. All controls mutate fictional in-memory state. The passcodes and names are fictional review values. No personal data, seed dump, avatar, photo, downloaded font, gradient, chart, added religious imagery, or external motion dependency ships.

## Visual fidelity

The visual anchor is the immutable type-refinement copy of the original Open Bulletin direction. The prototype preserves its page geometry, white and blue palette, blue nearest-service card, pale later cards, rounded surfaces, whitespace, typography pairing, and responsive phone and desktop composition. The only approved large-text adjustment is a modest heading and time reduction in the type-refinement anchor.
