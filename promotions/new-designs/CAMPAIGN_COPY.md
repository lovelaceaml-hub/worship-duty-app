# Worship Duties — Campaign Copy

## Master launch message

### Headline
**Three new ways to see Worship Duties.**

### Supporting line
Same schedule. Same duties. Same song plans. Choose the view that feels best to you.

### Member announcement
Worship Duties now includes three new appearance choices: **Prism**, **Navy & Cream**, and **Aurora**. Each design gives the app a different look and layout while keeping the same live schedule, your assigned duties, permissions, and Presider song plans.

Open **Appearance** in the app, choose a design, and explore. Your selection applies to the browser or device you are using, and you can switch again anytime.

### Short announcement
New Worship Duties designs are here: **Prism**, **Navy & Cream**, and **Aurora**. Same schedule and duties — now with a view you can choose. Open **Appearance** to try one.

### CTA options
- Choose your view
- Explore the new designs
- Open Appearance
- Try a new look

# Prism

### Primary headline
**See the month. Focus the service.**

### Subhead
A crisp, calendar-first workspace built for quick scanning and clear service details.

### Feature copy
Prism puts the calendar at the center, keeps service information close at hand, and uses a bright blue visual system to make dates, duties and active selections easy to follow.

### Short caption
**PRISM** — Calendar-first clarity. Fast scanning, focused details, and a clean modern workspace.

### One-line pitch
For members who want the schedule to feel clear, quick and precise.

### CTA
**Appearance → Prism**

---

# Navy & Cream

### Primary headline
**Warm. Refined. Familiar.**

### Subhead
A composed service timeline with cream surfaces, navy structure, burgundy actions and restrained gold details.

### Feature copy
Navy & Cream gives Worship Duties a calmer, more traditional visual rhythm while keeping the information easy to scan. Rich contrast and warm surfaces make the schedule feel polished without changing how your duties work.

### Short caption
**NAVY & CREAM** — A warm, refined view for schedules, duties, Presider and Manage.

### One-line pitch
For members who prefer a polished, quieter and more classic presentation.

### CTA
**Appearance → Navy & Cream**

---

# Aurora

### Primary headline
**More space to plan.**

### Subhead
A spacious, dynamic workspace with focused navigation and room for the full schedule.

### Feature copy
Aurora reshapes Worship Duties into a broader workspace with a dedicated navigation rail, a dynamic schedule area and distinct layouts for My Duties, Presider and Manage. It is designed to feel expansive while keeping the same live information underneath.

### Short caption
**AURORA** — Spacious navigation, dynamic schedule views, and a modern workspace feel.

### One-line pitch
For members who want the app to feel more like a full planning workspace.

### CTA
**Appearance → Aurora**
